Design Principles

PrincipleGuideline
canvasAnchor every page on the cream canvas (#faf9f5). Pure white reads as "any other AI tool".
displayCopernicus serif for every display headline. Weight 400, never bold. Negative letter-spacing non-negotiable.
coralReserve coral for primary CTAs and full-bleed callout cards. Don't paint accent moments coral elsewhere.
rhythmAlternate cream → cream-card → dark-mockup bands. The cream-to-dark contrast is the brand's pacing mechanism.
no-cool-blueCoral is the brand voltage. Don't use cool blue or saturated cyan as a brand accent.
elevationColor-block first, shadow rare. Most depth comes from cream-vs-dark surface contrast.
